# Campaign brief: Fernway 2.0 launch

SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Fernway Labs is a fictional client.
Client: Fernway Labs. Product: Fernway, a trail-running and hiking app.
Prepared by the client marketing team, 19 October 2026.

## Background
Fernway 2.0 adds offline trail maps, live check-ins with friends and route sharing.
The app has 400,000 installs, mostly in the US. Version 2.0 is our first push into the UK.

## Objectives and KPIs
- 60,000 new installs in the first 30 days after the iOS launch.
- Blended CPI at or under $3.00 in the US and £2.40 in the UK.
- Day-7 retention of 25% or better for paid cohorts.
- Keep the App Store rating at 4.6 or higher through the launch.

## Key dates
- Agency kickoff: Monday 26 October 2026.
- Creative concepts to our CMO for sign-off by Friday 6 November.
- iOS launch: Tuesday 1 December 2026. This is a hard date, tied to our press moment.
- Android launch: one to two weeks after iOS. The exact date is TBC by our engineering team.
- Day-30 results review with our leadership: week of 4 January 2027.

## Markets and audiences
- US (70% of paid budget) and UK (30%).
- Core audience: trail runners and hikers aged 25 to 45.
- UK copy must use British spelling and metric units.

## Budget
- Paid media: $180,000 over the first six weeks from launch.
- Creator partnerships: budget TBC, pending finance approval in early November.

## What we need from the agency
1. Paid user acquisition on Meta, TikTok and Apple Search Ads, live on launch day.
2. ASO refresh: new screenshots, a 30-second app preview video, new keywords around "offline trail maps", and a UK store listing.
3. A launch press release plus embargoed briefings with tech and outdoor media, so coverage lands on launch day.
4. Ten creator partnerships with trail-running creators, subject to budget.
5. A launch landing page and three SEO articles about offline navigation and trail planning.
6. Measurement: our MMP is installed, but SKAN conversion values and the new 2.0 events (route_saved, checkin_started) are not set up yet.
7. A live dashboard from launch day, a weekly report every Monday, and a day-30 report against the KPIs above.

## Constraints
- Our legal team must approve every public claim, ad and press material at least 5 working days before it goes live.
- No safety guarantees in any copy. For example, never say the app "keeps you safe".
- Our team is out for US Thanksgiving, so nothing can need our approval on 26 or 27 November.
- We are open to your recommendation on Black Friday weekend spend if CPMs spike.

## Other notes
- Brand guidelines v3 apply (on the shared drive).
- Our CMO wants a Spanish-language version of the paid social creative for US Hispanic audiences.
- Primary contact: our Head of Growth.
