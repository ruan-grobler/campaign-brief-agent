# Campaign brief: Ledgerly receipts launch

SYNTHETIC EVAL DATA. Ledgerly is a fictional client.
Ledgerly is a B2B expense app for small businesses in the US. It is launching receipt scanning that files expenses automatically.

## Goals
- 2,500 new business sign-ups in the first 45 days.
- 400 demo requests from companies with 20 to 200 staff.

## Dates
- Feature launch: Wednesday 2 December 2026, with a live webinar that afternoon.
- Press release distributed on launch day.

## What we need
1. LinkedIn and Google Search campaigns for finance managers, live on launch day.
2. A launch press release and outreach to small-business and fintech trade press.
3. A product demo video, LinkedIn ad creative and a webinar landing page.
4. Two SEO articles on expense automation and a webinar registration email sequence.
5. Lead tracking from ad click to demo request in the CRM, and a weekly pipeline report.

## Constraints
- Every claim about time saved must be approved by the client's legal team 5 working days before use.
- Budget: $90,000 for paid media. Webinar platform is already licensed.
