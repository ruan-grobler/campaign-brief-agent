# Campaign brief: Kindred Market holiday push

SYNTHETIC EVAL DATA. Kindred Market is a fictional client.
Kindred Market is an online marketplace for handmade gifts in the US. This is its holiday gifting campaign.

## Goals
- $1.2M in gift sales between 23 November and 21 December 2026.
- Return on ad spend of 4.0 or better.

## Dates
- Campaign goes live: Monday 23 November 2026.
- Last day for guaranteed Christmas delivery messaging: Friday 18 December 2026.

## What we need
1. Shopping and Performance Max campaigns plus Meta catalogue ads, live on 23 November.
2. A gift guide landing page with 12 curated collections, and matching ad creative.
3. Ten gift-guide creators on Instagram and TikTok, budget $40,000.
4. A press pitch to gift-guide editors at consumer magazines, landing before Black Friday.
5. Daily revenue and ROAS reporting during the campaign.

## Constraints
- Shipping cut-off messages must update automatically after 18 December.
- Creator content needs client approval before it goes live, and disclosure on every post.
