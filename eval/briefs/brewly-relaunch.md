# Campaign brief: Brewly app relaunch

SYNTHETIC EVAL DATA. Brewly Coffee Co. is a fictional client.
Brewly is a UK coffee-subscription app. Version 3 adds pause-and-skip and a new rewards wallet.

## Goals
- 15,000 new subscribers in the 8 weeks after relaunch.
- Cost per subscriber at or under £18.

## Dates
- Relaunch in the App Store and Google Play: Tuesday 19 January 2027.
- Creative concepts to the client by Friday 4 December 2026.

## What we need
1. Paid social on Meta and TikTok, live on relaunch day, £60,000 over 8 weeks.
2. New store screenshots and a 20-second preview video for both stores.
3. Three short how-it-works videos for organic social and the store listings.
4. Tracking for the new events (subscription_paused, reward_redeemed) and a weekly dashboard.

## Constraints
- No press release or media outreach this time. The client handles comms in-house.
- All copy in British English. No health claims about caffeine.
- The client's marketing team is out from 21 December 2026 to 4 January 2027.
